# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.90

## New

- The Licence card now has a **Get a licence** button that opens the StreamBridge Pro checkout, plus links to the terms and the privacy notice.

## Changed

- StreamBridge now accepts only licence keys for StreamBridge Pro from the SlothBloom store. Keys from any other store are refused. Everything stays free during the beta; a licence still turns nothing on or off.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
