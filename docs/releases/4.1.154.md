# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.154

## Fixed

- Updating StreamBridge while SprigCast was open could fail with "StreamBridge did not stop within 15 seconds", and the update was cancelled. StreamBridge stopped, but its kept-open connection to SprigCast held the program open for up to 5 more minutes. StreamBridge now closes that connection when it stops, so it exits straight away and the update goes through.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
