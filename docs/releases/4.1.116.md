# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.116

## Also in this update

- New: the creator's own PCs always run StreamBridge Pro, with no licence key, no online check and no end date. The Licence card shows StreamBridge Pro as active on those PCs.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
