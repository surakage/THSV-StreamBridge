# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.152

## Changed

- Tray notifications say plainly what happened and what to do. For example, "Streaming app is slow to answer" names the slow app, how long it took and your limit, and "Streaming app connection changed" says which app StreamBridge is linked to now.

## Fixed

- The TikTok LIVE reminder said "OBS is streaming" or "OBS stopped streaming" even when you stream with SprigCast (or Meld or Streamlabs). It now names the app you actually stream with.
- The TikTok LIVE reminders showed even when TikTok wasn't part of the stream, for example "End your TikTok LIVE" after a Twitch-only test stream with TikTok LIVE Studio closed. StreamBridge now asks LIVE Studio first. "Go LIVE on TikTok" only shows while LIVE Studio is open and not live yet, and "End your TikTok LIVE" only shows while it is live (or paused). If LIVE Studio can't be asked, the reminder shows as before, so a running LIVE is never forgotten.
- StreamBridge's Windows notifications showed "NotifyIconGeneratedAumid_..." as their name, with a small, blurry picture and Windows' generic blue info icon. They now show "THSV StreamBridge" and a sharp Village badge.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
