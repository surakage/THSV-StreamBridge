# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.166

## Fixed

- YouTube dropped out of the viewer counter (Live count) during a stream. Streamer.bot sends YouTube's viewer count only when a number changes, so on a steady stream it can go an hour without an update, and the counter dropped any count older than 20 minutes. A platform that is still live now keeps its last count until it goes offline. Counts from a platform that is no longer live still clear after 20 minutes.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
