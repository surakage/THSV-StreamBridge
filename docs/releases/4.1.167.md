# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.167

## Fixed

- In Critter Catch, the next critter to wander in after a catch showed only its name, with no picture. A catch hides the critter inside the Jar; when that was the visit's last throw, the overlay cleared the visit but kept the critter's picture hidden, so every later critter was drawn invisibly until the page was reloaded. Clearing a visit now also brings the picture and name plate back for the next one.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
