# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.91

## New

- The licence card now explains what the free plan leaves off and when a new or ended Pro licence takes effect (the plan is read once when StreamBridge starts, so nothing switches off mid-stream). The free plan limits stay switched off during the beta.

## Changed

- Extension source, update and revocation links now point at the public releases page.
- The download no longer includes source maps or type files, which makes it a little smaller.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
