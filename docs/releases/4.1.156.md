# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.156

## Fixed

- With SprigCast, the Go Live step (the Starting Soon Stream Deck key and other go-live buttons) sometimes only changed the scene. SprigCast's Stream doctor holds a go-live that comes from another app whenever it has a serious warning, even a passing one such as "the preview runs at 42 of 60 fps", and then nothing starts. Go Live now asks once, waits 3 seconds, and if SprigCast still isn't live it asks again with SprigCast's "Go live anyway". It also logs that the Stream doctor held it, so you can look in SprigCast for why. A missing stream key, or no place switched on, still stops it.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
