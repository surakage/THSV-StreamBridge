# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.98

## New

- Make starter scenes on the Overlays page. One click makes four ready-made OBS scenes (StreamBridge Starting Soon, Live, Be Right Back and Ending), each holding the overlays that suit it from the extensions you have turned on. Overlays that are already in OBS are shared into the new scenes, not copied, so their settings stay in one place. Your own scenes are never changed, and it refuses while you are live.
- A Start all streaming tools button on Start here, next to Ready to go live?, so Streamer.bot, StreamBridge and your broadcast app start with one click from the first page.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
