# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.163

## Fixed

- Raid Scout could leave the stream running after the ending flow. When Twitch never confirmed the raid and the ending ad had already finished, the stream end was due at once; StreamBridge's timer refuses a wait shorter than one second, so the stop step was never scheduled and the stream stayed live (seen on SprigCast on 2026-11-23, where the stream had to be ended by hand). Raid Scout now waits at least one second for any step, and a new stream clears an ending left unfinished by the last one.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
