# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.123

## Fixed

- The Stream Launch Countdown showed an empty box after its browser source reloaded (a scene collection switch, a SprigCast garden switch, or a refresh) while it was waiting for live. StreamBridge now keeps the last countdown and shows it again at once, until the countdown is hidden. The same goes for the other on-screen timers, such as Ad Break Companion, Stream Break & End Guard and Streamer Punishments.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
