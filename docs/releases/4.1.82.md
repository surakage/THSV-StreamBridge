# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.82

## Changed

- When StreamBridge takes too long to shut down, the log now names the part that was still running, and any extension that needs more than half a second to stop. This is to find what slows down restarts and updates.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
