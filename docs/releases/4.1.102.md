# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.102

## New

- When Windows Firewall is blocking phones, the card shows an Allow phones through Windows Firewall button. It opens only the phone port, only to your home network, after you choose Yes on the Windows prompt.

## Changed

- The phone card says home network instead of Wi-Fi, since the PC can be on a cable. It now says when a phone last reached the PC (so you can tell whether the phone gets through at all), and it has tips for when the phone says the site can't be reached: type http://, turn off mobile data, avoid guest Wi-Fi, and turn off client isolation on the router.

## Fixed

- On the Control Panel on your phone card, the address and the pairing code now sit on their own lines in boxes, instead of running into the text around them.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
