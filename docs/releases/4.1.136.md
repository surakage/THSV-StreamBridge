# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.136

## Changed

- When a raid clip ends or is stopped, the window capture is hidden before the Edge window closes, so its last picture is never left showing.

## Fixed

- On SprigCast builds that can catch one program's sound (feature `app_audio.pids`), the raid clip's "StreamBridge Raid Clip Sound" row now catches only the Edge window StreamBridge opened. Sound from your own Edge tabs is no longer heard on stream while a raid target's clip plays. Older SprigCast builds keep the 4.1.135 behaviour.

## Also in this update

- New: once SprigCast's Web pages can play H.264 (feature `browser.h264`), Raid Scout plays the raid target's clip with Twitch's player right in its page, and the Edge window and still picture are no longer used. Nothing to change on your side when that SprigCast build arrives.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
