# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.85

## New

- About 470 status messages, confirmation questions and small labels in the Wizard and on the extension cards (for example "Restart StreamBridge to apply saved settings.", copy and save messages, position names and overlay appearance labels) are now translated into Spanish, German, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
