# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.129

## Fixed

- The Streamer Punishments tracker stayed on screen after its Preview button or overlay test step ended. Hiding a preview now hides the tracker too, and any real tracker, timer or labels come straight back.

## Also in this update

- New: a preview of the punishment grace countdown (Preview mode "countdown", and the overlay test step `addon:thsv.streamer-punishments:countdown`), so a Punishment Countdown page set to `tracker=off` can be checked without a real punishment.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
