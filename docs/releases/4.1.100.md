# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.100

## New

- Under Bridge updates, with Install updates by themselves turned on, StreamBridge says when an update is ready and waiting for your stream to end, and when the last automatic update happened.
- What's new links to the full release notes for the version you just installed.
- A Performance setting for the Emote Wall (Low, Normal, High). Low keeps fewer emotes on screen and turns off animated GIF emotes, for weaker PCs.
- Help on the Licence card for moving to a new PC, and a Manage my licence link to the store's customer page, where you can find a lost key or change or cancel your plan.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
