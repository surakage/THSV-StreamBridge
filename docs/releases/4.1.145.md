# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.145

## Fixed

- A Random Clip Player clip that started silent on Starting Soon (or that started just before a scene change reached StreamBridge) stayed silent after you switched to Be Right Back or another scene with clip sound. The clip that is playing now follows the scene: it unmutes when you leave a muted scene and mutes when you enter one, without restarting. This works in SprigCast's clip video source, the Raid Scout window sound and the clip pages in OBS.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
