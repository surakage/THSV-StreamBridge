# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.142

## Fixed

- SprigCast's Stream doctor could refuse Go Live because the **StreamBridge Clip Video** or **StreamBridge Raid Clip Video** source had no video file while no clip was playing. StreamBridge now ships a tiny blank placeholder video (one see-through frame) and keeps it in those sources whenever no clip plays: "Set it up for me" makes them with it, and they go back to it after each clip ends, fails or is stopped, so the source never sits empty or on a clip the cache has since removed. Sources made by an earlier version are filled once when StreamBridge connects to SprigCast (never in the middle of a clip). Nothing in the layout changes.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
