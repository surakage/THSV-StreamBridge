# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.125

## Changed

- Scene pickers list SprigCast's blooms tidily. Your main blooms come first, then each of your sections (the ones you mark with separator blooms such as "🎚 Capture Scenes 🎚") from A to Z, then everything else. Blooms are A to Z inside each group, ignoring the emoji at the start. Separator blooms and sprigs are no longer listed. Scene roles keep their own group at the end.
- A scene picker without its own app setting now lists the scenes of the streaming app you picked on Start here, and SprigCast can be picked in the scene list pickers.

## Fixed

- Separator scenes such as "🎚 Main Scenes 🎚" are hidden from the OBS scene pickers too, and scenes sort by name instead of by their emoji.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
