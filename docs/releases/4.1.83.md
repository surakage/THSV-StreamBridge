# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.83

## New

- The setup steps, removal steps and health check descriptions of every extension (the "Before you begin" guide) are now translated into Spanish, German, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. That is about 295 more pieces of text per language.

## Changed

- Each release page now lists what is new, changed and fixed, taken from this changelog, instead of only the version number.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
