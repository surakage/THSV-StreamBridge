# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.88

## New

- The Wizard now shows a small "Pro" label on the extensions that will be part of StreamBridge Pro after the beta, and the licence card says what Pro will cover (a third platform and the Pro extensions). Nothing is locked: every feature keeps working for everyone during the beta.
- The free and Pro list lives in one place in the app (`bridge/services/licence-plan.ts`), with the rules for later: two platforms on the free plan, safety and accessibility extensions always free, and no change in the middle of a stream.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
