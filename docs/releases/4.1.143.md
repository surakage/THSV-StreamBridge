# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.143

## Fixed

- With SprigCast as your streaming app, TikTok LIVE automation never pressed Go LIVE in TikTok LIVE Studio. Before going live on TikTok, StreamBridge makes sure the virtual camera TikTok shows is on, and it always asked OBS, so with OBS closed it gave up and showed the reminder instead. It now asks your streaming app: SprigCast turns on SprigCast Camera, and OBS turns on OBS Virtual Camera as before. Meld, Streamlabs, or no streaming app picked still use OBS.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
