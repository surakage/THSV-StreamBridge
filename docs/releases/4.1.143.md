# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.143

## Fixed

- With SprigCast as your streaming app, TikTok LIVE automation never pressed Go LIVE in TikTok LIVE Studio. Before going live on TikTok, StreamBridge makes sure the virtual camera TikTok shows is on, and it always asked OBS, so with OBS closed it gave up and showed the reminder instead. It now asks your streaming app: SprigCast turns on SprigCast Camera, and OBS turns on OBS Virtual Camera as before. Meld, Streamlabs, or no streaming app picked still use OBS.
- **Random Clip Player** didn't start when you went live while already on a clip scene (for example Starting Soon), if that scene was picked before StreamBridge last started. StreamBridge only learns the scene from a scene change, so it didn't know which scene was on air. The go-live signal now names the scene on air for every streaming app (OBS, SprigCast, Meld and Streamlabs), and clips start from it.
- The **Ad Break Companion** countdown stayed on screen after the stream ended in the middle of an ad break (or with one coming up). It now clears as soon as your streaming app stops streaming or Twitch goes offline. Previews you run while offline are not affected.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
