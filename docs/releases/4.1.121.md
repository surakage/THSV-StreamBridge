# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.121

## Changed

- Streamer Punishments' Switch Scene helper follows your streaming app: OBS and SprigCast through Streamer.bot's OBS connection, Streamlabs and Meld through their own. Set Filter says plainly that Streamlabs and Meld filters are done by StreamBridge itself.
- Stream Break & End Guard's scene helper accepts SprigCast and switches its scene through Streamer.bot's OBS connection (SprigCast's OBS door).
- Raid Scout's Go Live and Stop All helpers notice when Streamer.bot's OBS connection is really SprigCast. They start or end SprigCast's stream (every place switched on in SprigCast) and skip the Aitum plug-in steps that only exist in OBS.
- The Bridge Launcher's connection checks say "SprigCast" when Streamer.bot's OBS connection reaches SprigCast, instead of "OBS".

## Also in this update

- New: every Streamer.bot action StreamBridge runs is told which streaming app you picked (OBS, SprigCast, Streamlabs or Meld) as `thsvStreamingApp`, so its C# uses the right connection instead of assuming OBS.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
