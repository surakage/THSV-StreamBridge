# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.165

## Fixed

- Features kept acting live after the stream ended. When the streaming app stops, StreamBridge now ends the live session on every platform, even one that never reports going offline, so every extension stops: break and end timers, rotations, chat reminders and scene switches. Seen on SprigCast on 2026-11-25: the TikTok live was ended by hand, so LIVE Studio refused StreamBridge's End LIVE and TikTok never reported offline. Stream Break & End Guard kept counting, posted a break warning to TikTok chat and switched to the break scene three minutes after the stream ended. Stream Break & End Guard also stops on its own as soon as the streaming app stops. A platform that reports offline later is ignored as a repeat.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
