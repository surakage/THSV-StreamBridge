# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.103

## Changed

- The phone card's tips now say what to do when Chrome on Android shows ERR_TOO_MANY_RETRIES: open the address in Samsung Internet or Firefox, which reach the PC normally.

## Fixed

- The Control Panel on a phone stayed on the Unlock screen and its button did nothing. Phones open it over plain http, where the browser leaves out a feature the page needed at start-up, so none of its scripts ran. It now works without it.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
