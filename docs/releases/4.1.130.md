# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.130

## Fixed

- A Streamer Punishments page with `?tracker=only` stretched the tracker panel over the whole page, so a full-canvas page showed a huge, mostly empty panel. The panel now keeps its normal size in its saved corner.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
