# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.95

## Changed

- Reward Timers redraw half as often and only re-measure their panel when a timer starts or ends, with the bar still sliding smoothly.
- Goal Bars show each goal's percentage, a row of coloured dots with each platform's share when more than one platform helped, and a soft glow once a goal is reached.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
