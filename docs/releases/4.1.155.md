# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.155

## Fixed

- StreamBridge held two connections to SprigCast instead of one: the scene connection and the kept-open request connection from 4.1.151. Requests now always use the scene connection when it is open (also after SprigCast moved to another port), and the request connection closes as soon as the scene connection is up and its last answer is in.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
