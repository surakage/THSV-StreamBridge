# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.84

## New

- The labels, buttons and explanations on the extension cards (setup summaries, the moderation dashboard, quote library, jukebox controls, follower history, action approvals and overlay previews) are now translated into Spanish, German, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. That is about 500 more pieces of text per language.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
