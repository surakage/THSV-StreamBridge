# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.128

## Fixed

- In the overlay test sequence, test cheers and gift subscriptions now show at once. Before, they waited 5 seconds to be combined like real ones, so they appeared late and pushed the next test alert back. Real cheers and gift subscriptions still combine.

## Also in this update

- New: each overlay test step reports `visibleMs`, about how long it stays on screen, so a screenshot can be timed while it shows. Emote Wall explosions and fireworks are over in 2 to 4 seconds.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
