# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.127

## Changed

- The Scene roles group in scene pickers is clearer. It is headed "Scene roles: follow what you set in Features > Scene roles", and each entry reads like "Main live scene (follows Scene roles, now 🔴 Gameplay)". Picking a role keeps working when you rename or swap scenes, which is why it is listed apart from your scenes.

## Fixed

- In every extension's scene list picker (for example "Only on these scenes"), picking a detected scene jumped straight back to "Choose a detected scene…", so Add scene did nothing. The pick now stays, and Add scene adds it.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
