# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.161

## Fixed

- In Critter Catch, a Jar throw could still leave the critter looking pale or washed out, as if its colours had changed. The swirl into the Jar no longer brightens the critter at all (it fades a little instead), so nothing can stay on it after a catch or a miss.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
