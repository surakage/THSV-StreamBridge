# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.141

## Changed

- **Random Clip Player** and **Raid Scout** only start from a scene change while your streaming app is streaming. This works the same in OBS, SprigCast, Streamlabs and Meld. While the app is offline, closed or not answering, switching to a clip scene (or the ending scene) does nothing: no clips, no SprigCast Clip Video or Raid Clip Video, and no Edge raid clip window. If you go live while already on a clip scene, clips start then. When the stream stops, clips stop as before.
- StreamBridge's go-live and stream-stopped detection now follows the streaming app you picked (it used to ask OBS only). SprigCast, Meld and Streamlabs are checked every 10 seconds; OBS still reports right away. Anything that waits for "stream started" or "stream stopped" (for example TikTok LIVE automation and live recovery) now follows SprigCast, Meld and Streamlabs too. If the app is closed for 30 seconds mid-stream, the stream counts as ended.

## Also in this update

- Unchanged on purpose: your own presses still work offline, so you can test. That is Start clips in the Control Panel (and the Streamer.bot Enable action) for clips, and Suggest, Finish Stream and Cancel for Raid Scout.
- New for extension makers: `context.streaming.live` (no permission needed) is true while the streaming app is streaming, false when not, and undefined before StreamBridge has asked it once.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
