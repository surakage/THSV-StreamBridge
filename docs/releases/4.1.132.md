# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.132

## Changed

- Chat First on a vertical page (`overlayOrientation=vertical`) wraps its names into as many columns as the page is wide, instead of one tall column. Five names now fit a short, wide vertical box at full size instead of being shrunk; a narrow page still stacks them in one column.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
