# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.114

## Changed

- StreamBridge licences now come from Polar instead of Lemon Squeezy, which stops taking payments on March 31, 2027. Paste a Polar licence key into the Licence card to activate it; one key works on two PCs. Nothing is locked before 5.0. A test licence saved from Lemon Squeezy during the beta is set aside.
- Get a licence on the Licence card opens the Polar checkout with all three plans, and Manage my licence opens the SlothBloom customer portal on Polar.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
