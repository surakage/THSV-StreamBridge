# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.118

## Changed

- Automatic updates now wait while your own streaming app streams or records (OBS, SprigCast, Meld Studio or Streamlabs Desktop, as picked under Scene connections), and the line names that app instead of always saying OBS.

## Fixed

- Under Bridge updates, the line below "Install updates by themselves" now shows the version you're running and when it was installed, whether it updated by itself, through the installer or by hand. Before, it kept showing the last automatic update, even when a newer version had been installed another way.
- Facebook is in the "Where do you stream?" list on Get ready to stream, matching the Main platform list. Ticking it turns on your saved Facebook Page connection; if no Page is connected yet, it says to connect one first.
- Facebook now counts towards the free plan's two platforms like the others, once the free plan is enforced at 5.0. Nothing changes before then.

## Also in this update

- Translated: the new update and Facebook lines, in all nine Wizard languages.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
