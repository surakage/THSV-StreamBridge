# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.87

## New

- In other languages, the setup guide sentences on the extension cards that contain bold names, commands or links (for example the YouTube key steps for Village Jukebox and the Xbox and PlayStation sign-in steps for Category Pilot) are now translated in all eight languages, about 160 pieces of text per language. Action names, commands and product names stay in English so they match Streamer.bot.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
