# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.109

## Changed

- One streaming app choice for the whole Wizard. The app picked under Extensions and the Streaming app on the Overlays and widgets pages are now the same setting, so changing one changes the other.
- The Overlays, Stream widgets, Chat overlay, Alerts and Live captions pages use your app's words too (for example "browser layer" in Meld, "web page source" in SprigCast). Sentences that compare apps stay as written. "Not seen in OBS yet" only shows when you use OBS, since only OBS reports the overlays it loads.
- App wording now works in all nine Wizard languages, not just English, and the streaming-app picker, the Check panel, its how-tos and its results are translated.
- Raid Scout, Random Clip Player and Face Filters show the setup step for your app (for example Keep running when hidden in SprigCast, or Shutdown source when not visible in Streamlabs) instead of the OBS one.

## Fixed

- The translated setup steps added in 4.1.108 now appear in the Wizard (they were saved but not built into the language files).

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
