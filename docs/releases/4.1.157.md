# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.157

## Fixed

- In Critter Catch, a critter that broke free of a Jar came back looking washed out, as if its colours had changed, and stayed that way for the rest of its visit. The swirl into the Jar brightens the critter, and that brightness stayed on after it popped back out. The critter now keeps its own colours after a miss.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
