# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.131

## Fixed

- On a vertical Punishment Countdown page (`overlayOrientation=vertical`), the compact countdown kept its saved corner offset, so on a page sized to the card it was pushed partly off the page. It now fills the page like the other vertical timers.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
