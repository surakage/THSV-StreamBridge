# THSV StreamBridge 4.1.93

## New

- The licence card lets you pick which two platforms stay connected on the free plan. The choice applies the next time StreamBridge starts, so nothing changes during a stream. Free plan limits stay switched off during the beta.

## How to update

Open the Wizard, press Check for updates and then Update now. Your settings and extensions are kept, and it never installs while you are live.
