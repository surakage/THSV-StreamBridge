# THSV StreamBridge is ready for everyone

THSV StreamBridge is a free, open-source companion for multi-platform streamers on Windows. It connects Twitch, Kick, YouTube, a Facebook Page and TikTok to Streamer.bot and your streaming app, so you set up chat, alerts, commands and features **once** instead of once per platform.

It runs entirely on your own PC. Nothing is hosted online, and it never asks for your platform passwords.

## What you get

- **One chat** from every platform: an on-stream chat box, and a dock you can reply from.
- **Alerts** for follows, subs and memberships, gifts, Bits, Super Chats, tips, raids and TikTok gifts, with your own sounds and images.
- **One set of commands and timed messages** that work on every platform, plus a page where viewers can see the commands.
- **Rewards everywhere:** Twitch and Kick channel point rewards, and StreamBridge points with `!buy` for YouTube, Facebook and TikTok.
- **Stream flow:** a Starting Soon countdown, break and end-of-stream timers, scene automations, a raid finder and a clip player.
- **Community features:** polls, giveaways, a prize wheel, quotes, check-ins, first chatters, Streamer Punishments, Face Filters, a Magic 8-Ball, and more.
- **Accessibility and language:** live closed captions of your voice, chat and event captions, chat translation, and translation of your own voice for viewers who ask. See [Accessibility features](../guide/accessibility.md).
- **A guided Setup Wizard** in your browser, with a Setup checklist that tells you exactly what each feature still needs.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- [Streamer.bot](https://streamer.bot/) 1.0.7 or newer.
- OBS Studio, Meld Studio or Streamlabs Desktop for overlays (optional).
- TikFinity for TikTok (optional).

No Node.js, database or coding. Everything StreamBridge needs comes in the download.

## Good to know

- **Windows only.** StreamBridge, Streamer.bot and the launcher tools are Windows programs.
- **Streamer.bot does the platform connections.** StreamBridge works through it, so your accounts stay connected in Streamer.bot.
- **Kick rewards** have no cooldown or limit of their own; StreamBridge enforces the limits you set and tells viewers.
- **Updates are one click** from the wizard, verified against the official GitHub release, and blocked while you are live.

## Get started

1. Download the latest release from the [Releases page](https://github.com/surakage/THSV-StreamBridge/releases/latest).
2. Follow the [Quick start](../guide/quick-start.md). It takes about 30 minutes, from download to your first alert on stream.
