# Code of Conduct

StreamBridge is built by and for streamers and their communities. We want everyone who uses it or helps with it to feel welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to streaming tools.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing someone else's private information, including tokens, keys or stream details, without permission.
- Spamming issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) and say it is a conduct report.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
