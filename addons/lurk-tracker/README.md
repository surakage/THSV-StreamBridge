# Village Lurk Tracker

Enable Viewer Foundation and this add-on. Match its lurk command. A viewer starts a lurk with !lurk; their next chat or another recognized command ends it automatically. Repeat lurks do not add extra visits. Offline events and Bridge shutdown end active lurks. Downtime is excluded after restart.

Monthly rankings use America/Chicago. Recaps are archived locally before reset and appear under Monthly Village Recaps in the Bridge. Points, identity links, and lifetime totals remain intact. Simulations and ignored bot accounts never enter the rankings.
